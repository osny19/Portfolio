Projects

A selection of projects I have worked on.

Age of Aliens

A fast-paced 2D tower defense game developed in Unity. Players defend their base against waves of aliens by strategically placing and upgrading turrets. The project focuses on gameplay systems, enemy behavior and progression. Winner of the LiU Game Awards 2025.

A playable demo is available on Steam. Age of Aliens on Steam

Technologies: Unity, C#

The Ghost is Behind You

A 3D horror game developed using OpenGL and C++. The player navigates a haunted house while avoiding a ghost that is only visible through mirrors.

Technologies: OpenGL, C++, GLSL

Rubiks cube controller

A Godot-based 2×2×2 Rubik’s Cube game controlled through hand gestures. Uses Python and MediaPipe for real-time hand tracking.

Technologies: Godot, Python, MediaPipe
